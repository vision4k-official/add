How to Tutorials

Follow these quick steps to get started.

Video: how to load your playlists

A one-minute guide to adding M3U and Xtream playlists.

Install the app

Open the Downloads page, press “Download App” and install Vision 4K on your device. On Android TV or Fire TV you may need to allow installs from unknown sources.

Find your MAC Address and Device Key

Open Vision 4K on your device. Both values are shown on the activation screen of the app.

Pair your device

Go to Manage Playlists, enter the MAC Address and Device Key exactly as shown in the app, then press “Pair Device”.

Add a playlist

After pairing, press “+ Add Playlist”, choose M3U or Xtream Codes, fill in the details and save. You can manage up to 5 playlists per device.

Update the app

After making changes on this page, open Vision 4K and press UPDATE so your playlists are refreshed.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

My playlist does not show in the app. What should I do?

After making changes on this page, open Vision 4K and press UPDATE. Also check that your playlist details are correct.

How do I extend my access?

Pair your device, open Activate Device, choose your period, pay with Binance and tap “I paid – notify admin”. Our team confirms and updates your expiry date.

Does Vision 4K provide channels or playlists?

No. Vision 4K is a media player only. You must add your own content that you are authorized to access.

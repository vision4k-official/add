How to Tutorials

Follow these quick steps to get started.

Video: how to load your playlists

A one-minute guide to adding M3U and Xtream playlists.

Install the app

Open the Downloads page, press “Download App” and install Vision 4K on your device. On Android TV or Fire TV you may need to allow installs from unknown sources.

Find your MAC Address and Device Key

Open Vision 4K on your device. Both values are shown on the activation screen of the app.

Pair your device

Go to Manage Playlists, enter the MAC Address and Device Key exactly as shown in the app, then press “Pair Device”.

Add a playlist

After pairing, press “+ Add Playlist”, choose M3U or Xtream Codes, fill in the details and save. You can manage up to 5 playlists per device.

Update the app

After making changes on this page, open Vision 4K and press UPDATE so your playlists are refreshed.

How to pay with USDT

It takes about 3 minutes. No crypto yet? You can buy USDT with a card.

  1. Open this page on your device, go to Activate Device, choose your period and tap Pay now. You will see an address and an exact amount in USDT.

  2. Buy USDT on an exchange such as Binance: tap Buy Crypto and pay with your credit or debit card.

  3. Send it: go to Wallet > Withdraw > Crypto > USDT and paste the address from the payment page.

  4. Choose the network BSC (BEP20).

  5. Enter the exact amount shown. Add the small network fee so the full amount arrives.

  6. Confirm. Your device activates automatically within a few minutes. Open the app and tap Reload.

Important: any network other than BSC (BEP20) means lost funds.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

My playlist does not show in the app. What should I do?

After making changes on this page, open Vision 4K and press UPDATE. Also check that your playlist details are correct.

How do I extend my access?

Pair your device, open Activate Device, choose your period, pay with Binance and tap “I paid – notify admin”. Our team confirms and updates your expiry date.

Does Vision 4K provide channels or playlists?

No. Vision 4K is a media player only. You must add your own content that you are authorized to access.

Legal Terms

Please read before using Vision 4K.

Media player only

Vision 4K is a media player. It does not provide, sell or host any channels, playlists or subscriptions.

Your content

You are solely responsible for the content you add. Use only content you are authorized to access.

Privacy

The MAC Address and Device Key are used only to link your device to your playlists. For privacy, saved playlist details are shown only inside the Edit window of an unlocked playlist.

Data we collect

Vision 4K Pro does not require an account. To work, the app uses your device identifiers (MAC Address and Device Key), the playlist or service details you enter yourself, your app version, and your approximate country (derived from your IP address). This data is used only to run the app, to link your device to your playlists and to manage your access period. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Data deletion

You can ask us to delete your data at any time. Send an email to vision4k.support@gmail.com with your MAC Address and Device Key, and tell us whether you want some or all of your data removed. We will process the request and confirm by email. You can also remove your playlists yourself inside the app (Settings).

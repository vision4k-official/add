Vision 4K - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Vision 4K (Vision 4K Pro / Vision 4K Free) is a media player application. The app does not include, provide or sell any channels, streams or content. Users add their own playlists or content sources.

Information we collect

  • Device identifiers used for activation and licensing (device ID and device key).
  • App version and the date/time the app was last used.
  • IP address and the approximate country derived from it.
  • Contact details you choose to send us (for example by email or the contact form).

How we use it

To activate and manage the app, to provide support, to prevent abuse and to understand how many devices use the app. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Your content

Playlist addresses and settings you enter are used by the app to play your content. We are not responsible for the content of third-party sources.

Playlist addresses and the activation panel

In the Pro edition, playlist addresses that you add can be sent to our server so that they can be managed together with your activation (for example when you add a list on our website using your device MAC address and key). In the Free edition, playlists stay on your device. We do not store or provide any media content.

Integrity check

To protect against modified copies of the app, the app sends a fingerprint (hash) of its signing certificate together with the device identifiers to our server. It does not contain personal information.

Advertising (Free edition only)

The Free edition shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may use the Advertising ID of your device and may ask for your consent in regions where the law requires it. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID in your device settings. The Pro edition contains no ads and no advertising SDKs.

Security

Communication between the app and our server is encrypted. We do not ask for your name, address, phone number or payment details inside the app.

Your rights

You can ask us what data is linked to your device, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing the address below with your device ID.

Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. To request deletion of the data linked to your device, email us at the address below with your device ID.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Vision 4K - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Vision 4K (Vision 4K Pro / Vision 4K Free) is a media player application. The app does not include, provide or sell any channels, streams or content. Users add their own playlists or content sources.

Information we collect

  • Device identifiers used for activation and licensing (device ID and device key).
  • App version and the date/time the app was last used.
  • IP address and the approximate country derived from it.
  • Contact details you choose to send us (for example by email or the contact form).

How we use it

To activate and manage the app, to provide support, to prevent abuse and to understand how many devices use the app. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Your content

Playlist addresses and settings you enter are used by the app to play your content. We are not responsible for the content of third-party sources.

Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. To request deletion of the data linked to your device, email us at the address below with your device ID.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
